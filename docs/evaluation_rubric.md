# Assessment rubric — Surface-EMG lab practicals (emgteach)

> A rubric to assess the student deliverables of the
> [lab practical guide](lab_practicals.md). Each criterion is scored **0–10** and
> weighted; the final mark is the **weighted average**. The performance-level
> descriptors below help grade consistently. Adjust the weights to your course.
>
> The central deliverable is the **PDF report** the application generates, which
> carries the calibration with its repetitions, the contraction table, the summary
> cards and, in the pair, the co-activation table. Most of what is assessed is
> there; what is not there is the interpretation, which is why it weighs more.

## Criteria and weights

| # | Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set-up and recording quality | 20 % |
| 2 | Calibration of the maximum and selection of what is analysed | 15 % |
| 3 | Use of the application and reading of its results | 20 % |
| 4 | Physiological interpretation | 30 % |
| 5 | Report and presentation | 15 % |

**Final mark = Σ (criterion score × weight).**

## Performance-level descriptors

Levels: **Insufficient (0–4)** · **Sufficient (5–6)** · **Good (7–8)** ·
**Excellent (9–10)**.

### 1. Set-up and recording quality (20 %)

- **Insufficient.** Electrodes off the belly or across the fibres; recordings
  with saturation, a flat channel or artefacts that prevent reading.
- **Sufficient.** Acceptable placement; usable recording with some artefact.
- **Good.** Placement measured from the bony landmarks, reference over bone,
  clean recording; in the pair, channel separation below 25 %.
- **Excellent.** In addition, the artefacts that occurred are identified in the
  report with their cause, and the cross-talk is discussed with its figure.

### 2. Calibration of the maximum and selection of what is analysed (15 %)

- **Insufficient.** The «Task maximum» card says «not a maximum» and the
  calibration was not repeated; or the whole file was analysed, calibration
  included, when the exercise asked for the task.
- **Sufficient.** Acceptable calibration (task below 150 %); repetitions and
  fragments accepted without review.
- **Good.** Maximum made against the table and held; weak repetitions discarded
  with judgement; fragments limited to what the exercise asked for.
- **Excellent.** In addition, the task maximum lies between 90 and 125 %, and the
  report explains which repetitions were discarded and why.

### 3. Use of the application and reading of its results (20 %)

- **Insufficient.** Practical chosen that does not match the set-up; cannot find
  the numbers in the contraction table or the cards.
- **Sufficient.** Correct practical; reads the cards and the table with help.
- **Good.** Chooses the practical and the exercise that fit the question; reads
  the per-contraction table, the cards and the right panels (PSD for the
  spectrum, 7 for fatigue, 9 and the co-activation table in the pair, the
  force-velocity study in kinematics); uses % MVC where it belongs.
- **Excellent.** Autonomous use; distinguishes what the application measures from
  what it interprets; knows what «not reported» and «not conclusive» mean and why
  they are answers rather than faults.

### 4. Physiological interpretation (30 %)

- **Insufficient.** Incorrect or absent reasoning; confuses amplitude with
  frequency; compares millivolts between people or between muscles.
- **Sufficient.** Basic, mostly correct interpretation; limited depth; does not
  place the figures against the reference ranges.
- **Good.** Relates amplitude to recruitment and firing rate, the fall of MDF to
  conduction velocity, % MVC to comparability, the Jonsson levels to risk; judges
  the figures against the ranges.
- **Excellent.** In addition, argues the unexpected (an MDF out of range, a grip
  with high co-activation, an electromechanical delay that changes with load)
  with testable physiological hypotheses, and tells a finding from a montage
  problem.

### 5. Report and presentation (15 %)

- **Insufficient.** Disorganised; no PDF report or tables; no conclusions.
- **Sufficient.** PDF report attached; the guide's tables filled in; brief
  conclusions.
- **Good.** Clear report; the figures quoted match the PDF; the questions are
  answered with the numbers at hand.
- **Excellent.** In addition, precise language, well-chosen figures and a
  conclusion that links what was measured to the course's physiology.

> **Tip.** For a quick formative check two things from the PDF suffice: the
> **«Task maximum»** card (if it is red, nothing else holds) and one question
> about a number in the contraction table («why is this MDF higher than that
> one?»). The first says whether they could calibrate; the second, whether they
> understand what they measure.
